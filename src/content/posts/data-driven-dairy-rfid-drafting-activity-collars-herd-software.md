# Data-Driven Dairy: Operating RFID Drafting Gates, Activity Collars & Herd Software

Modern commercial dairy farming relies heavily on precision livestock technology to monitor health, manage heat detection, and streamline drafting without slowing down cow flow. Implementing hardware like Electronic Identification (EID/RFID) ear tags, automated pneumatic drafting gates, and rumination/activity collars requires operators who understand both software interfaces and physical mechanical hardware. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers, candidate instruction incorporates digital record systems and automated herd management tools alongside traditional stockmanship.

---

## 1. Electronic ID (RFID) & Automated Pneumatic Drafting Operations

Drafting individual cows for veterinary treatment, artificial insemination (AI), or foot trimming after milking must happen seamlessly as animals exit the parlour.

[ RFID Reader Scan ] ---> [ Herd Software Match ] ---> [ Pneumatic Gate Signal ] ---> [ Auto-Draft / Main Herd Split ]


### Candidate Operational Standards

* **Antenna & Sensor Alignment:** Training candidates to monitor RFID panel readers, ensuring exit lanes remain clear of electrical interference and mud buildup that can cause missed tag reads.
* **Pneumatic Gate Maintenance:** Inspecting air compressor pressure lines, solenoids, and optical safety eyes on drafting gates to ensure rapid, quiet gate swings that prevent cow balking or injury.
* **Manual Override Protocols:** Mastering rapid manual override controls during power cuts or software glitches to maintain uninterrupted cow flow out of the shed.

---

## 2. Activity Collars, Rumination Monitoring & Health Alerts

Wearable sensor technology (collars and ear tags) provides real-time data on estrus behavior and rumination drops signaling early metabolic or systemic illness.

| Sensor Indicator | Data Pattern Detected | Candidate Action Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **High Activity Spike** | Increased movement, mounting behavior, and reduced eating time | Drafts cow for AI timing check; verifies standing heat symptoms |
| **Rumination Drop (>20%)** | Significant decrease in daily chewing/rumination minutes | Drafts cow for physical exam (temp check, ketone test, mastitis check) |
| **Low Activity + Low Feed** | Prolonged inactivity, head-down posture | Immediately logs cow for veterinary evaluation and isolation |

## 3. Herd Management Software & Daily Data Entry

Precision technology is only as valuable as the accuracy of the data entered into the system. Candidates are trained in standard herd software interfaces (e.g., MINDA, LIC, VELOS, DelPro, or uniform-ag).
