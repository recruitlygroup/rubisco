# Herd Genetic Selection, Genomic Testing & Breeding Index Optimization

Accelerating genetic gain within a commercial dairy herd requires transitioning from traditional pedigree recording to data-driven genomic selection. By evaluating breeding values early in an animal's life and selecting high-ranking sire teams aligned with regional breeding indexes, farm managers enhance milk solids efficiency, improve health and fertility traits, and optimize herd longevity.

---

## 1. Understanding National & Regional Breeding Indexes

Breeding indexes prioritize economic traits, allowing farmers to rank animals according to their projected profitability per lactation.

[ Genomic Sample Collection ] ---> [ Breeding Index Ranking ] ---> [ Sire & Dam Mating Allocations ] ---> [ Targeted Genetic Gain ]


### Core Breeding Index Components

* **Production Traits:** Weightings on total Fat ($kg$) and Protein ($kg$) yields to drive milk solids output per hectare.
* **Fertility & Health Traits:** Weightings on calving interval, somatic cell score (SCS), and mastitis resistance to lower culling rates.
* **Functional Conformation:** Traits of importance including teat placement, udder depth, chest width, and foot angle to support structural durability.

---

## 2. Genomic Testing Protocols for Replacement Heifers

Genomic testing analyzes tissue samples (typically ear notches or hair follicles) to assess an animal's DNA profile, providing high accuracy for lifetime performance predictions at a young age.

| Genomic Testing Phase | Operational Action | Strategic Outcome |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Sample Collection** | Tissue sampling tag (TST) applied at birth or weaning | Eliminates parentage errors; identifies genetic defects early |
| **Data Evaluation** | Upload DNA data to national genetic database | Receives genomic breeding value (gBV) with $>65\%$ reliability |
| **Selection Sorting** | Rank replacement heifers into top, mid, and bottom tiers | Directs high-value sexed semen to top performers; beef bull semen to bottom tier |

## 3. Strategic Artificial Insemination (AI) Mating Plans

Integrating sexed semen with beef cross-breeding optimizes genetic progress in replacement heifers while generating high-value beef calves from lower-ranking cows.

---

## 4. Managing Inbreeding & Genetic Diversity

Rapid genetic progress using popular AI bulls increases the risk of inbreeding depression, which lowers fertility and calf vigor.

* **Inbreeding Thresholds:** Maintain herd inbreeding coefficient levels strictly below **6.25%** across all planned matings.
* **Automated Sire Matching:** Utilize computerized mating programs to cross-reference farm herd sire allocations against dam pedigrees, automatically blocking inbreeding conflicts before breeding season begins.

Leveraging genomic data and structured breeding plans accelerates genetic gain, producing resilient cows tailored to farm production systems.
