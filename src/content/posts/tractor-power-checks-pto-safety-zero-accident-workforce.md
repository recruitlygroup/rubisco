# Tractor POWER Checks & PTO Safety: Training a Zero-Accident Machinery Workforce

Machinery accidents and mechanical breakdowns represent significant financial losses and operational delays on modern commercial dairy farms. International farm employers require workforce candidates who prioritize safety, respect machinery investments, and perform consistent daily equipment checks. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers in Nepal, tractor safety and preventative maintenance are embedded as mandatory core competencies before any student steps onto a production farm.

---

## 1. The Pre-Start Routine: Rigorous POWER Checks

Before starting any tractor or loader, CTEVT-trained candidates execute the standardized **POWER** pre-start inspection protocol to identify wear, fluid leaks, or tire damage before minor issues become catastrophic failures.

[ P - Petrol/Fuel ] ---> [ O - Oil Levels ] ---> [ W - Water/Coolant ] ---> [ E - Electrical System ] ---> [ R - Rubber & Tires ]


### Candidate Pre-Start Execution Standards

* **Oil & Fluid Dynamics:** Checking engine dipsticks, transmission fluid sight glasses, and hydraulic reservoir levels on flat ground prior to engine startup.
* **Radiator Cleanliness:** Clearing debris, chaff, and silage dust from radiator screens to prevent engine overheating during heavy loader or PTO work.
* **Tire & Hardware Integrity:** Inspecting sidewall integrity, pressure, and wheel nut tightness, ensuring pedal locks are engaged for road transit.

---

## 2. Mandatory PTO Guarding & Rotational Safety

Power Take-Off (PTO) shafts operating at **540 to 1,000 RPM** present extreme entanglement hazards if operated without functional protection. CTEVT practical modules enforce zero tolerance for damaged or missing PTO guards.

| Safety Parameter | Student Assessment Standard | Farm Operational Benefit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PTO Guard Shroud** | Inspects 100% yellow plastic cover for cracks, tears, or missing sections | Prevents fatal or severe operator entanglement hazards |
| **Safety Restraint Chains** | Verifies anchor chains are secured to tractor/implement frames | Keeps protective sleeve stationary while inner shaft spins |
| **Universal Joint Greasing** | Applies greasing schedules every 8–10 operating hours | Eliminates bearing seizure and heavy vibration damage |
