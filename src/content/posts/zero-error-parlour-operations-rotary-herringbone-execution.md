# Zero-Error Parlour Operations: High-Speed Rotary & Herringbone Execution for Commercial Farms

Maintaining high cow throughput while preserving milk quality and udder health is the operational backbone of large-scale commercial dairy farms. Whether managing a 80-bail rotary or a high-capacity swing-over herringbone system, parlour efficiency depends on consistent operator routines, calm stock movement, and strict adherence to milking hygiene protocols. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers, candidates undergo practical training designed to achieve seamless, zero-error execution in high-throughput parlour environments.

---

## 1. Standardized Pre-Milking Preparation & Oxytocin Timing

Consistency in pre-milking routines maximizes natural oxytocin release, ensuring rapid milk letdown, fast peak flow rates, and minimal machine-on time per cow.

[ Calm Cow Entry ] ---> [ Stripping & Pre-Dipping ] ---> [ 30s Contact Time ] ---> [ Individual Paper Wipe ] ---> [ Cluster Attachment (60-90s) ]


### High-Throughput Execution Benchmarks

* **Teat Stimulation & Timing:** Candidates are trained to attach clusters within **60 to 90 seconds** of initial teat contact, timing attachment precisely with peak hormonal milk letdown.
* **Foremilk Inspection:** Stripping foremilk on every cow allows early detection of clinical mastitis before milk enters the main bulk milk receiver line.
* **Hygiene Compliance:** Enforcing the "one towel, one cow" rule using single-service paper wipes prevents cow-to-cow transfer of contagious pathogens like *Staphylococcus aureus*.

---

## 2. Rotary & Herringbone System Mechanics

Different parlour designs demand distinct operator positioning, rhythm, and safety awareness to maintain steady cow flow without causing stress or bottlenecks.

| Parlour Type | Key Operator Routine | Training Focus & Efficiency Metric |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Rotary Parlour** | Fixed-station operation (Teat Prep, Cup Attacher, Post-Dipper) | Maintains consistent entry pace (5–7 seconds per bail) without stopping the platform |
| **Herringbone Parlour** | Batch entry, rapid line-up, and simultaneous cluster placement | Smooth backing-gate management and rapid, air-tight cluster attachments |
| **Swing-Over System** | Alternating side cluster transfer across central milk line | Minimizes idle cluster hanging time; aligns long milk tubes cleanly to prevent squawking |

## 3. Cluster Alignment, Teat Dipping & CIP Hygiene Protocols

Poor cluster alignment causes liner slips, air squawking, and incomplete milk-out, increasing the risk of intramammary infection.
