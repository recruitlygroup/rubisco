# CTEVT-Certified Dairy Workforce: Skill Standards, Practical Competencies & Global Placement

Sourcing reliable, technically skilled dairy personnel is a persistent operational challenge for farm owners and managers worldwide. To bridge the global agricultural labor shortage, Nepal’s Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT)—a government-regulated national authority—delivers standardized, hands-on agricultural training designed to meet international farm management benchmarks.

---

## 1. Government-Regulated Vocational Framework (CTEVT & NSTB)

Similar to international vocational qualification systems like TESDA in the Philippines, CTEVT operates as Nepal’s governing body for technical education, skill standards development, and national competency assessments.

[ CTEVT Curriculum Alignment ] ---> [ Practical Hands-On Training ] ---> [ NSTB Skill Assessment ] ---> [ Certified Job-Ready Candidate ]


### Institutional Rigor & Quality Control

* **Government-Standardized Curricula:** CTEVT programs combine intensive theoretical coursework with practical application in pasture management, herd health, parlour mechanics, and safety compliance.
* **National Skill Testing Board (NSTB) Assessment:** Graduates undergo independent, practical skill evaluation by the NSTB to verify hands-on execution before earning professional credentials.
* **Institutional Credibility:** As a government-regulated system, CTEVT-affiliated institutions enforce strict attendance, practical hours, and competency benchmarks across all training modules.

## 2. Practical Competency Core: What Our Graduates Can Do

Students complete comprehensive, applied farm modules ensuring seamless integration into modern dairy operations without requiring extensive baseline training on arrival.

| Operational Area | Certified Competency & Execution Level |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Milking Operations** | Proficient in rotary, herringbone, and swing-over parlours; trained in CIP systems, milk quality compliance, and teat hygiene protocols. |
| **Pasture & Feeding** | Experienced in pasture plate metering, daily break allocation, rotation calculation, and TMR wagon loading sequences. |
| **Calf Rearing & Health** | Trained in colostrum management, automated feeder operation, neonatal scours treatment, and basic obstetric assistance. |
| **Machinery & Safety** | Skilled in daily tractor POWER checks, safe PTO shaft operation, basic hydraulic maintenance, and biosecurity enforcement. |

## 4. Delivering Job-Ready Talent to International Dairy Markets

Graduating students possess not only technical dairy capabilities but also a strong work ethic, adaptable problem-solving skills, and basic operational English for clear farm communication.

* **Target Alignment:** Candidates are tailored for dairy systems across New Zealand, Australia, Europe (Ireland, Austria, UK), and the Gulf region.
* **Employer Customization:** Training centers can incorporate farm-specific Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) into late-stage practical modules prior to candidate departure.

Partnering with CTEVT-certified training providers gives international dairy employers direct access to a stable, highly competent, and ethically sourced technical workforce.
