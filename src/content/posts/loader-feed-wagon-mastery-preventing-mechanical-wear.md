# Loader & Feed Wagon Mastery: Training Dairy Workforce to Prevent Mechanical Wear

Front-end loaders and Total Mixed Ration (TMR) feed wagons are among the most heavily utilized assets on modern commercial dairy farms. High-hour daily usage in silage pits and feed alleys means rough operation quickly leads to worn clutches, burnt hydraulic pumps, broken auger shear pins, and premature tire degradation. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers, candidate instruction focuses on smooth machine operation, precise feed loading sequences, and preventative equipment care.

---

## 1. Smooth Loader Operation & Hydraulic System Protection

Rough cycling of tractor front-end loaders strains the front axle assembly, hydraulic seals, and transmission clutches. CTEVT practical modules train operators to handle high-density silage and round bales with care.

[ Gradual Hydraulic Lever Control ] ---> [ Smooth Crowd & Lift Cycles ] ---> [ Low-Gear Yard Travel ] ---> [ Controlled Dump Rate ]


### Machinery Care Execution Standards

* **Clutch & Transmission Preservation:** Training operators to select low creep gears when pushing into silage faces or manure piles, eliminating high-RPM clutch riding and wheel spin.
* **Hydraulic Pressure Relief:** Enforcing smooth joystick movement rather than dead-heading hydraulic rams against extreme pressure limits, extending hydraulic pump lifespan.
* **Counterweight Balance:** Verifying rear ballast or heavy counterweights are fitted before lifting dense silage bales to prevent excessive stress on the tractor's front axle bearings.

---

## 2. Feed Wagon (TMR) Operation & Auger Shear Pin Management

Improper loading sequences or sudden Power Take-Off (PTO) engagement under full load can snap shear pins, twist drive shafts, or jam vertical and horizontal mixing augers.

| Operational Phase | Student Execution Benchmark | Mechanical Protection Result |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **PTO Engagement** | Engages PTO at idle engine RPM (**<1,000 RPM**) before bringing up to operating speed | Prevents sudden torque shock to tractor transmission and feed wagon gearbox |
| **Loading Order** | Loads long-fiber straw/hay first, followed by silage, then concentrates/liquids | Ensures even mixing without jamming augers or overloading drive chains |
| **Load Monitoring** | Monitors digital weigh-scale indicators continuously during loading | Prevents hopper overloading beyond rated axle tonnage capacity |

## 3. Feed Alley Delivery & Brake / Tire Wear Prevention

Uniform feed distribution along feed pads requires consistent forward tractor speed without riding the foot brakes or scrubbing tires against concrete bunks.
