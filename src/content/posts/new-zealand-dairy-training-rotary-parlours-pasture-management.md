# New Zealand Dairy Training Standards: Rotary Parlours, High-Throughput Cupping & Spring Pasture Management

New Zealand’s dairy sector is built on high-throughput, low-cost pastoral farming. Operating commercial herds ranging from 400 to over 1,500 cows requires a distinct operational skill set compared to indoor barn systems. During peak spring calving, platform operators face strict time constraints: maintaining high cow throughput while protecting udder health, managing teat hygiene, and accurately allocating daily grass breaks across extensive pasture paddocks.

## 1. High-Throughput Rotary Parlour Operations (50+ Bail Platforms)

On New Zealand pastoral farms, 50 to 80-bail internal and external rotary parlours are the industry standard for handling large herds efficiently. Candidates are trained to maintain platform pace without compromising animal welfare or sanitation.

[ Entry Platform Pace ] ---> [ Teat Preparation & Strip ] ---> [ Rapid Cup Attachment ] ---> [ Automatic Teat Spray ] ---> [ Exit & Draft ]
### Candidate Performance Benchmarks for NZ Parlours

* **Cupping Speed & Routine Discipline:** Candidates are trained to achieve a target cupping speed of **$4–5\text{ seconds per cow}$**, allowing a single operator to manage **250 to 400+ cows per hour** alongside automated cup removers (ACRs).
* **Pre-Milking Teat Hygiene:** Mastering the "strip, spray, wipe" sequence to identify early clinical mastitis and maintain low Bulk Milk Somatic Cell Counts (BMSCC) under tight platform rotation speeds.
* **Back-Off & Retention Straps:** Operating pneumatic bail restraints and managing stubborn cows on the platform entry point without interrupting turn-table momentum.

## 2. Pasture Allocation & Rising Plate Meter Grass Budgeting

New Zealand's pastoral economy relies on perennial ryegrass and white clover swards. Dairy managers require personnel who understand pasture cover calculations to achieve target post-grazing residuals.

\text{Available Grass (kg DM/ha)} = \text{Pre-Grazing Cover} - \text{Target Post-Grazing Residual (1,500 kg DM/ha)}
| Practical Task | Student Execution Protocol | NZ Farm Operational Value |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Pasture Auditing** | Walks paddocks using a rising plate meter (RPM) or RPM app to record compressed sward height | Generates accurate weekly feed wedges for the farm manager |
| **Break Allocation** | Calculates daily square-meter area needed per herd based on target $\text{kg DM/cow/day}$ allocation | Prevents under-feeding or over-grazing past the **1,500 kg DM/ha** residual benchmark |
| **Break Fencing** | Sets up pigtail standards, temporary polywire breaks, and geared reels efficiently on farm tracks | Enables precise 12-hour or 24-hour grass shifts without wire entanglements |
