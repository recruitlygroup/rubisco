## 1. Ireland: Spring-Calving Logistics & Pasture Management

Irish dairy production is internationally recognized for its seasonal, grass-based model aligned with Teagasc research benchmarks. Candidates destined for Irish farms undergo targeted practical modules to support peak seasonal workloads.

### Essential Skills for Irish Dairy Operations

* **Compact Calving & Youngstock Rearing:** Training in rapid calf triage, optical Brix refractometer colostrum scoring (ensuring $>22\%$ quality), safe esophageal tube feeding, and early scours isolation.
* **Pasture Allocation & Break Fencing:** Measuring pasture mass with rising plate meters (RPM) to maintain target post-grazing residuals of $1\text{,}400 - 1\text{,}500\text{ kg DM/ha}$.
* **Heat Detection & Breeding Preparation:** Identifying estrus signs using tail paint, scratch cards, and collar data to support target 6-week submission rates.
