About Rubisco Tech: Engineering the Future of Dairy Farming in Nepal

Rubisco Tech Private Limited is a newly incorporated company headquartered in Suryabinayak-04, Bhaktapur, Nepal, built around a single conviction: Nepal's dairy sector doesn't need more manual labor — it needs better systems and better-trained people.

We operate under the tagline "Innovation through Tech & Science" — and everything we build, from software to syllabus, is designed to close the gap between how Nepali dairy farms currently run and how they could run with the right technology and the right hands on the ground.

The Problem We Exist to Solve

Nepal's dairy industry sits at an inflection point. Demand for milk and dairy products keeps climbing, yet the operational backbone of most farms hasn't kept pace:

Skills shortage: Very few workers are formally trained in modern milking technique, relief milking, or herd management — most learn informally, inconsistently, and without safety or hygiene standards.
Technology gap: Farm management is still largely manual — record-keeping, health tracking, and yield monitoring happen on paper or not at all.
Investor hesitation: People and institutions who want to build tech-enabled dairy farms from scratch often don't know where to start — or who to trust to build and staff it.
Employer frustration: Farm owners struggle to find workers who are actually job-ready on day one.

Rubisco Tech was founded to sit directly at the intersection of these problems.

What We Do

We operate on two connected fronts:

1. Dairy-Tech & IT Solutions

We design and build technical software, automation tools, and digital systems that solve real operational problems for farms and dairies — covering areas like:

Farm and herd data management
Milk yield and quality tracking
Automation for repetitive dairy operations
Digital infrastructure for farms scaling up from manual to modern operations
2. Agricultural Training Institute

We run practical, skills-first training programs that produce genuinely job-ready dairy talent, covering:

Relief milking technique and hygiene protocol
Milking operations on modern equipment
Herd management fundamentals
Modern farm technology — bridging traditional dairy work with digital tools

The two arms reinforce each other: our training produces the skilled talent our tech is built for, and our tech gives our trainees real systems to work on — not theory in a vacuum.

Who We're Building This For

For Investors If you're planning to build a tech-enabled dairy farm from the ground up, we bring both the technical infrastructure and the trained workforce pipeline needed to make it operational — not just a concept.

For Employers & Farm Owners If you're tired of hiring untrained labor and starting from zero, our training institute is built to hand you dairy workers who already understand relief milking, herd handling, and modern equipment.

For Students & Trainees If you want a practical, hands-on path into dairy operations and modern agri-tech — not just classroom theory — Rubisco Tech's training programs are built around real farm skills that translate directly into employment.

Why Now

We were incorporated in September 2026 as Rubisco Tech Private Limited — a fresh start, built specifically to meet this moment in Nepal's dairy economy: rising demand, a widening skills gap, and a technology gap that most of the industry hasn't addressed yet. We're not retrofitting an old business model — we're building for where dairy in Nepal is headed.
