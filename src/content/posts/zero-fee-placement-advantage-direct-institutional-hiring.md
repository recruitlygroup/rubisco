# The Zero-Fee Placement Advantage: Direct Institutional Hiring vs. Recruitment Agency Margins

Sourcing dependable international agricultural talent traditionally involves third-party recruitment agencies charging substantial placement fees, finder commissions, and hidden service markups. For commercial dairy farm owners, these middleman costs add thousands of dollars per hire without guaranteeing that candidates possess genuine practical skills. By partnering directly with government-regulated technical institutes like CTEVT-affiliated centers in Nepal, farm employers eliminate financial markups while gaining direct access to verified, skill-tested graduates.

## 3. Direct Customization & Video Interview Access

Working directly with an educational institute allows employers to participate in candidate selection and request farm-specific pre-departure training tailored to their operations.

* **Live Practical Video Audits:** Employers can view live video practical assessments or request customized practical skill testing (e.g., tractor hitching, rotary parlour prep) before signing contracts.
* **SOP Integration:** Training centers integrate employer-specific parlour software, herd protocols, and machinery routines into late-stage practical modules prior to candidate departure.

Bypassing commercial recruitment middleman channels directly lowers the Cost Per Hire (CPH) while dramatically raising candidate skill reliability on commercial dairy farms.
