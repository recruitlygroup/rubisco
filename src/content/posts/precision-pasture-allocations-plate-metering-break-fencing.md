# Precision Pasture Allocations: Plate Metering & Break-Fencing Mastery for Grazing Farms

In pasture-based dairy operations—such as those across New Zealand, Australia, Ireland, and the UK—maximizing milk solids per hectare depends entirely on precise forage allocation and maintaining high pasture quality. Over-allocating grass leads to wasted feed and poor post-grazing residuals, while under-allocating compromises daily dry matter intake (DMI) and milk production. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers, candidate instruction focuses on accurate pasture measurement, feed budgeting, and efficient temporary fencing routines.

---

## 1. Pasture Measurement: Rising Plate Meter Calibration & Auditing

Accurately calculating total standing biomass per hectare requires consistent measurement techniques across varying sward densities and seasonal conditions.

[ 30–50 Plonk Walk Trajectory ] ---> [ Compressed Height Reading (cm) ] ---> [ Equation Application ] ---> [ Cover Output (kg DM/ha) ]


### Plate Metering Execution Standards

* **Representative Sampling:** Training candidates to walk a standard "W" pattern across paddocks, taking 30–50 plonks while avoiding pugged areas, cow tracks, or dung patches.
* **Conversion Calibration:** Applying regional standard formulas (e.g., $\text{Cover} = \text{Height in cm} \times 140 + 500$) to accurately calculate average kilograms of Dry Matter per hectare ($\text{kg DM/ha}$).
* **Pre- and Post-Grazing Residual Targets:** Enforcing strict target checks—ensuring cows enter paddocks at optimal covers (**2,800–3,000 kg DM/ha**) and leave clean residuals (**1,500 kg DM/ha** or 7–8 clicks on a plate meter) to maintain sward quality.

## 2. Calculating Daily Break Allocations & Feed Budgeting

Translating pasture cover data into exact daily paddock allocations prevents pasture wastage and stabilizes herd energy intake.

| Calculation Step | Operational Formula / Parameter | Student Execution Target |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Available DM / ha** | $\text{Pre-Grazing Cover} - \text{Target Post-Grazing Residual}$ | Identifies net usable feed per hectare (e.g., $2,900 - 1,500 = 1,400\text{ kg DM/ha}$) |
| **Herd Daily Requirement**| $\text{Number of Cows} \times \text{Target DMI per Cow}$ | Calculates total daily intake requirement (e.g., $400\text{ cows} \times 17\text{ kg DM} = 6,800\text{ kg DM}$) |
| **Area Break Allocation** | $\frac{\text{Total Herd Requirement}}{\text{Available DM / ha}}$ | Determines precise area required per shift (e.g., $\frac{6,800}{1,400} = 4.85\text{ ha/day}$) |
