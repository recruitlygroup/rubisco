# Inside the CTEVT Practical Exam: How Students Pass NSTB Milking & Calf Audits

International dairy employers often wonder how technical qualifications awarded in Nepal translate to daily execution on commercial farms. Under the Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT), certification is not awarded based on written exams alone. Candidates must undergo rigorous, time-bound practical evaluations conducted by independent assessors from the National Skill Testing Board (NSTB). 

Here is an inside look at the exact practical audits candidates must pass to earn certification in milking parlour operations and neonatal calf care.

---

## 1. The NSTB Milking Parlour Hygiene & Execution Audit

During the practical milking assessment, students are evaluated on real dairy cows in fully operational parlour setups (herringbone/rotary). Assessors track the student's workflow, speed, animal handling, and adherence to Clean-In-Place (CIP) protocols.

[ Pre-Milking Teat Prep & Foremilking ] ---> [ Cup Attachment & Alignment ] ---> [ Automated/Manual Cluster Removal ] ---> [ Post-Milking Teat Dipping & CIP ]


### Milking Assessment Benchmarks

| Assessment Phase | Required Candidate Action | Failure Criteria |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Teat Preparation** | Applies pre-dip, strips foremilk to check for mastitis, and wipes dry with individual paper towels after 30 seconds contact time | Reusing towels across cows or failing to check foremilk |
| **Cluster Attachment** | Attaches teat cups within **60–90 seconds** of initial teat stimulation to leverage natural oxytocin release | Air squawking, twisted liner hoses, or improperly aligned clusters |
| **Post-Milking Sanitization** | Applies complete 100% teat coverage dipping; initiates CIP wash cycle with correct water temp logs | Incomplete teat dip coverage or skipping wash chemical dosing |

## 2. The NSTB Calf Health & Colostrum Audit

Youngstock rearing requires extreme attention to detail. In the NSTB calf care assessment, candidates are evaluated on neonatal resuscitation, colostrum quality testing, feeding hygiene, and early disease detection.

+-------------------+-----------------------------------------------+

## 3. Objective Evaluation: How Candidates Earn Passing Marks

Assessors use strict checklists aligned with National Occupational Skill Standards (NOSS). A candidate cannot pass by simply knowing the theory—they must demonstrate speed, safety, and correct technique in real-time.

* **Zero-Tolerance Safety Rules:** Any breach of animal welfare (rough handling) or operator safety (entering a bull pen unsafely, improper machine handling) results in immediate failure.
* **Hygiene Compliance:** Cross-contamination errors during milking or calf feeding instantly deduct pass-grade points.
