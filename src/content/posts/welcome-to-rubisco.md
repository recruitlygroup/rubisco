# Hello, World! Welcome to Rubisco 👋

Welcome to the official blog of **Rubisco**! We are an AgriTech IT company based in Nepal, dedicated to building modern software solutions, data tools, and digital platforms designed specifically for the agricultural ecosystem.

Named after *RuBisCO*—the critical enzyme at the heart of photosynthesis that drives life and growth on Earth—our mission is to serve as a catalyst for growth in Nepal's agricultural sector through technology.

---

## 💡 Who We Are

At our core, we are software engineers, product thinkers, and innovators passionate about solving real-world challenges. Nepal's agricultural sector forms the backbone of our economy, yet many farmers, supply chain partners, and agribusinesses still struggle with fragmented tools, lack of data access, and technological gaps. 

Rubisco was created to bridge this exact divide—bringing modern, reliable, and accessible IT solutions to agriculture.

---

## 🚀 What We Do

We build digital tools that make farming and agricultural management smarter, more efficient, and data-driven. Our work spans across:

* **Agri-Software & Platforms:** Custom web and mobile applications designed for farm management, supply chain tracking, and market linkage.
* **Smart Data & Insights:** Digitizing farm metrics, weather patterns, and crop monitoring to help growers make informed decisions.
* **Technology Infrastructure:** Combining software with modern hardware and IoT solutions tailored for Nepal's unique geographical and economic landscape.

---

## 📈 What to Expect From This Blog

This blog will serve as our open journal where we share:

1. **Company & Product Updates:** Behind-the-scenes look at the software tools and platforms we are building.
2. **Tech & Agri Insights:** Articles on modern software engineering practices, AgriTech trends, and practical digital solutions for farming.
3. **Founder Stories & Field Lessons:** What we learn directly from working with farmers, businesses, and tech partners across Nepal.
