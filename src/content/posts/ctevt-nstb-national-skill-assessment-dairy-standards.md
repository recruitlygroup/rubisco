# CTEVT National Skill Testing Board (NSTB) Standards: Standardized Dairy Assessment & Industry Readiness

Securing competent, verified agricultural personnel requires objective evaluation beyond paper qualifications. In Nepal, the **National Skill Testing Board (NSTB)**—operating under the government-constituted Council for Technical Education and Vocational Training (CTEVT)—serves as the official body for establishing occupational skill standards, conducting hands-on performance assessments, and issuing nationally recognized trade certifications.

---

## 1. Structure of National Occupational Skill Standards (NOSS)

The NSTB develops Occupational Skill Standards that define the exact practical steps, technical knowledge, and safety procedures required for an individual to perform competently in modern agricultural and dairy trades.

[ Pre-Assessment Gap Identification ] ---> [ Rigorous Practical Exam ] ---> [ NSTB Skill Certificate Issued ] ---> [ Zero-Fee Employer Placement ]


### NSTB Certification Levels Relevant to Commercial Farming

* **Level 1 (Entry-Level Trade):** Verifies basic operational competency, machine safety, and routine farm execution under direct supervision.
* **Level 2 (Craftsperson / Technician):** Verifies independent task execution, equipment maintenance, herd management monitoring, and field decision-making.
* **Level 3 (Senior Technician / Supervisor):** Verifies advanced troubleshooting, herd health diagnostics, supervisory leadership, and farm record management.

---

## 2. Practical Evaluation Methodology: How Students Are Tested

Unlike purely academic examinations, NSTB skill assessments are predominantly performance-based tests evaluated on real demonstration farms and fully equipped agricultural labs.

| Assessment Pillar | Practical Evaluation Standard | Verification Requirement |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Milking Mechanics** | Execution of rotary/herringbone prep, cluster alignment, teat dipping, and CIP chemical wash cycles | Zero procedural errors in sanitation or animal handling |
| **Animal Care & Health** | Subcutaneous/intramuscular injections, hoof trimming basics, newborn calf resuscitation, and scours rehydration | Safe handling compliance and correct dosage calculation |
| **Machinery Safety** | Tractor pre-start POWER checks, safe PTO shaft coupling, implement hitching, and hydraulic leak checks | 100% adherence to occupational health & safety protocols |
| **Pasture & Nutrition** | Pasture plate meter readings, daily allocation calculations, and TMR wagon loading sequences | Accurate yield measurement and feed allocation |
