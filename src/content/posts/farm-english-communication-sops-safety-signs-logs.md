# Farm English & Communication: Training Workers in SOPs, Safety Signs & Log Management

Clear communication is a fundamental requirement for farm safety, animal health, and smooth operational flow on international commercial dairy enterprises. When hiring international workforce candidates, farm owners and herd managers must be confident that workers can comprehend instruction, read chemical and machinery safety labels, execute written Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and maintain accurate daily farm logs. At CTEVT-affiliated agricultural training centers, functional English and farm record management are integrated directly into technical modules.

---

## 1. Applied Farm English & Operational Vocabulary

Language instruction at CTEVT training facilities focuses on practical agricultural English used daily in milking parlours, machinery sheds, and livestock handling yards.

[ Direct Oral Instructions ] ---> [ Immediate SOP Verification ] ---> [ Action Execution ] ---> [ Written Log Update ]


### Core Communication Modules

* **Technical Terminology:** Candidates are trained in standard English terminology for equipment parts (e.g., *liner, pulsator, claw, dip cup, PTO shaft, quick-coupler*), veterinary products, and feed types.
* **Instruction Comprehension:** Practical drills reinforce active listening—ensuring candidates can accurately carry out direct verbal tasks, ask clarifying questions when unsure, and report completion to farm supervisors.
* **Hazard & Safety Signage:** Mandatory reading comprehension modules cover workplace hazard symbols, chemical safety data sheets (SDS), personal protective equipment (PPE) requirements, and machinery warning labels.

---

## 2. Comprehending & Executing Written SOPs

Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) preserve consistency across large farming teams. CTEVT candidates are trained to read, follow, and adhere strictly to posted farm SOPs without taking unauthorized shortcuts.

| Farm Area | Written SOP Requirement | Candidate Execution Competency |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Milking Parlour** | Pre-dip, wipe, cluster attachment, and post-dip sequence | Follows timed hygiene steps precisely to maintain low bulk tank SCC |
| **Calf Shed** | Colostrum refractometer testing and feeding volumes | Measures Brix %, matches volume to weight, and records feed data |
| **Chemical Wash** | CIP alkali/acid water temperature and dosing ratios | Reads chemical dilution charts and adheres strictly to safety handling rules |
